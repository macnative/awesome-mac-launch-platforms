# Contributing

Thanks for helping keep this list accurate and useful for Mac developers. Before opening a PR, please read this.

## What belongs here

A platform, community, or newsletter qualifies if it lets someone **launch, share, or get discovered** for a Mac (macOS) app — free or paid. That includes:

- Launch/discovery platforms (Product Hunt-style, directories, marketplaces)
- Subreddits and other communities where posting your own app is allowed
- GitHub "awesome" lists that accept macOS app submissions via PR
- Newsletters that feature or review indie/Mac software

It does **not** include general social media (X, LinkedIn) or paid ad platforms (Google Ads, Meta Ads) — those are channels, not launch platforms, and belong in a different kind of list.

## How to add an entry

1. Fork this repo.
2. Add your entry to the correct table, **alphabetically** within its section.
3. Use this exact row format:

   ```
   | [Name](https://example.com) | Free / Paid / Freemium | One-line description of what it is and who it's for | Notes (e.g. approval required, native macOS only) |
   ```

4. Keep the description to one line, factual, no marketing language ("the best", "revolutionary", etc.).
5. Double-check the URL works and reflects the current submission flow — dead links get closed without merge.
6. Add a short `Last verified: YYYY-MM` note when you are correcting time-sensitive pricing or rules. It helps the next contributor know where to check first.
7. Open a PR with a short title, e.g. `Add [PlatformName] to Launch Platforms`.

## Updating an existing entry

Pricing and submission flows change often. If you spot something stale (a platform went paid-only, a subreddit banned self-promo, a link moved), open a PR fixing just that line — small, factual corrections are very welcome and fast to merge.

## What gets rejected

- Entries that are really just SEO backlink farms with no real audience
- Duplicate or near-duplicate platforms already listed
- Anything requiring payment just to be *listed* here (this list itself is always free)
- Broken or affiliate-wrapped links

## A note on changing information

Costs, queues, rules, and audience counts are time-sensitive. Please favor a direct submission or rules URL over a homepage, avoid estimates unless they are useful context, and say what changed in the PR description. A small correction is more valuable than a broad rewrite that cannot be verified.
